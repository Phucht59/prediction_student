# Phase 5 — MLP Gap Closing

**A. TABULAR RESIDUAL HYBRID CLEARLY EXCEEDS MLP ON INNER DEVELOPMENT**

The fair INNER protocol reverses the historical premise: MLP is below H0. H1 nevertheless improves H0 by `0.002079` and exceeds MLP by `0.006080`, with positive direction in 6/6 and 6/6 paired runs respectively. Worst-stage, PR-AUC, NLL, and Brier improve versus H0; ECE is slightly worse and remains disclosed.

Both temporal and residual paths materially contribute. Distillation does not improve H1 under stability and is rejected. Freeze H1; do not run outer evaluation until a separate final-evaluation phase authorizes one.
