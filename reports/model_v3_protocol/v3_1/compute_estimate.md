# Pre-run compute estimate

Full V3.1 has 40 Optuna selection studies × 20 trials × 3 inner folds = 2,400 inner candidate evaluations for M0–M3. Outer evaluation has 235 jobs and 14,852 prediction rows: M0–M3 contribute 200 five-seed jobs, M4 contributes 25 five-seed late-stage jobs, and deterministic B0 contributes 10 jobs. This is an estimate and not authorization to compute.
