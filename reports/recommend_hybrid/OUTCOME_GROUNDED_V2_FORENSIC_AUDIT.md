# V2 forensic audit

V2 evidence remains historical and unchanged. The audit found critical implementation and validity defects: reversed negative-control gate logic, post-fit rather than retrained label controls, hard-coded action eligibility/evidence, duplicated action proxies, a binary pseudo-residual, group-level rather than learner-cluster bootstrap, non-standard MAP@3, and an incomplete fairness audit.

These findings motivate V2.1. They do not change the V1 or V2 release statuses and do not reopen the V2 lockbox.
