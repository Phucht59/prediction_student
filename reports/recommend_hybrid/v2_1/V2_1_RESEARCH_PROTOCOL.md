# Outcome-grounded V2.1 research protocol

V2.1 is a corrective namespace. The prior V1 and V2 artifacts remain immutable. Evaluation uses nested grouped cross-validation over all three outer folds; outer fold 2 is not called a pristine lockbox. The primary estimand is offline predictive relevance, not causal effect.

Action-specific continuous future behavior and proximal signals are normalized by opportunity. Candidate actions are eligible only when the cutoff-safe course schedule and evidence support them. See `artifacts/recommend_hybrid/outcome_grounded_v2_1/PROTOCOL.json`.
