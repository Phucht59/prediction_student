# V2.1 actions and eligibility

Five scientific families are derived from cutoff-safe opportunity windows: assessment completion, study regularity, VLE engagement, quiz/retrieval practice, and content review. Eligibility requires an observed opportunity; action availability, need, opportunity count, deficit, evidence strength, and prerequisite status are recorded. Groups with fewer than two eligible actions are reported as `NOT_RANKABLE_POLICY_FALLBACK` and excluded from ranking denominators.
