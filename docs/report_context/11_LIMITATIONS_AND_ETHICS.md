# Limitations and ethics

## Limitations and threats to validity

- Only 395 records, one mathematics subject and one historical Portuguese
  secondary-school setting are represented.
- The data are not Vietnamese university data; external validity requires local
  validation.
- G2 contains dominant late-stage predictive signal; a complex model does not
  show added value over the G2 rule.
- The G1/G2 sequence length is two, so long-horizon temporal claims are invalid.
- Fairness slices are descriptive because subgroup support is small.
- Recommendation evaluation is structural; expert review and intervention
  effectiveness are not established.
- Live PostgreSQL migration and new DB-first verification remain pending.

## Ethical and deployment constraints

Predictions are not causal claims and must not label, rank or sanction students
automatically. Use human-in-the-loop review, data minimization, access control,
privacy safeguards and local institutional validation. Recommendations are
advisory and must not replace teacher or counselor judgment.
